Discover Tokyo

A mix of tradition and modern life

Tokyo offers busy shopping districts, historic temples, and many different dining experiences. It is a destination for travelers interested in Japanese culture and city exploration.

Places to explore

  • Shibuya for shopping and busy city streets.
  • Asakusa for Senso-ji Temple.
  • Akihabara for electronics and gaming culture.
  • Mt. Fuji for beautiful mountain viewing

Travel tips

Group nearby attractions together when planning your day. Use public transportation and allow time to explore beyond the main tourist attractions.
