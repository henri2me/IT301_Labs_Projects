Explore Historic Rome

Rome is a destination for travelers who enjoy ancient history, architecture, and Italian food. Build your visit around a few major sights while leaving time to explore the surrounding streets.

A Day of Sightseeing

  1. Morning: Ancient Rome

    Begin around the Colosseum and Roman Forum. These landmarks offer a starting point for exploring the city's ancient history.

  2. Afternoon: Explore on Foot

    Plan a walk around the Pantheon and nearby areas, with time for lunch and a break.

  3. Evening: Food and City Streets

    Finish with dinner and a walk through a neighborhood such as Trastevere.

Before You Go

Wear comfortable walking shoes and check official attraction websites for entry requirements before finalizing your itinerary.
